ABOUT / EXPERIENCE

From archaeology
to digital heritage.

I’m Sheak Mahfujur Rahman, an archaeologist and digital heritage researcher specialising in 3D documentation and spatial analysis.

At Kyoto University’s Maritime Asia Heritage Survey (2023–2026), I progressed from Spatial Data Technician to Digital Heritage Coordinator. I worked on complex photogrammetry and LiDAR processing, GIS outputs, and research-data publication across international heritage projects.

My earlier work in Bangladesh includes archaeological photography and the collaborative Paharpur Buddhist Vihara survey.
